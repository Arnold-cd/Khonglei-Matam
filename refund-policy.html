Refund Policy

Eligible Refund Situations

We provide refunds under the following circumstances:

  • Multiple / Duplicate Charges: If your bank account or payment method was billed more than once for the same transaction due to a technical error.
  • Technical Malfunction: If an in-app purchase was deducted from your account, but the digital service / Pro features were not unlocked after contacting support.
  • Unauthorized Transactions: In cases of proven fraudulent or accidental transactions reported within 48 hours of purchase.

Non-Refundable Circumstances

  • Change of mind after using the digital Pro features.
  • Partial refunds for unused days within an active monthly or multi-month billing cycle.
